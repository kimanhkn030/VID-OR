# ORISTAR reference-match frame system

## The Frame

Footage remains the full-bleed evidence layer. Text behaves like industrial
signage laid over the moving factory: compact, loud enough for mobile, but never
large enough to hide the operator, machine action or fixed ORISTAR logo.

## Composition Rules

- Keep primary copy upper-left or upper-middle and clear of the top-right logo.
- Use at most two headline lines; a third line is permitted only for compact lists.
- Fill roughly 60–75% of the safe text width and keep reading time under the VO beat.
- Cyan fill plus white stroke is the default. Invert to white fill plus cyan stroke
  only for hierarchy within the same beat.
- Green checks are proof punctuation, not a recurring bullet on every sentence.
- No panels, certification cards or factual graphics unless the source VO and image
  explicitly establish them.

## Motion Character

Mechanical, precise and quick: lines slide, words lock, checks draw. Entrances
decelerate into position; exits are shorter and accelerate away. Footage itself
never moves or receives a treatment.
