# Version 3 — Creative direction

## Concept angle

Turn the factory footage into a value-first industrial story: show the visible chain from material handling to machining, inspection, inventory, and dispatch. The rewritten narration may only make claims supported by the source voice-over or visibly demonstrated by the footage.

## Visual language borrowed from Video 4

- Full-bleed portrait footage; never use Video 4 footage.
- Bold condensed Vietnamese headline in the upper safe area.
- White primary text, Oristar blue emphasis, and one red registration rule.
- Small green confirmation chips and safety/quality pictograms only when the line is supported by the source.
- Mechanical, precise entrances: words snap or lock into place; checks pop on with a short tick; scene changes use a restrained directional whoosh.
- Keep the existing top-right Oristar mark unobstructed.

## Version 3 distinction

- Rebuild the story rather than mirroring the original edit order.
- Open with a useful outcome, then prove it with production and inspection evidence.
- Prefer three strong text beats in the 15-second proof over continuous subtitles.
- Use footage rhythm: quick evidence montage -> controlled hold -> clear promise.
- Use a newly generated neutral professional Vietnamese voice only after the transcript and rewritten script are approved. Do not imitate an identifiable speaker.

## Proposed 15-second rhythm

1. 0.0–2.2s — fast proof montage; hook lands in outcome language.
2. 2.2–6.2s — machine/process evidence; second line locks into place.
3. 6.2–10.8s — inspection/detail evidence; green check appears only with a sourced claim.
4. 10.8–15.0s — inventory/dispatch evidence; end on a concise brand promise drawn from the original narration.

## Design tokens

- Canvas: 1080x1920, 30 fps.
- Headline: Oswald 700 or Archivo Black 400, 92–118px depending line count.
- Supporting labels: IBM Plex Mono 700, 24–30px.
- Brand blue: sample from source/reference before final authoring.
- Accent red: sample from the Oristar mark/reference red rule.
- Confirmation green: use only for verified capability or standard.
- Text shadow/stroke: enough separation for bright factory footage; no opaque full-width card unless footage makes it necessary.

## Evidence gate

Each rewritten sentence must carry one receipt: an original transcript timestamp, a visible shot/time range, or both. Unverified capacity, certification, tolerances, customer segments, and superlatives are prohibited.
