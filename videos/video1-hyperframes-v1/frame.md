# Visual direction

Concept angle: an industrial field-label system rides over real factory footage,
landing short proof points with the same direct, energetic clarity as Video 4.

- Focal element: condensed uppercase Vietnamese headline, cyan-blue fill with a
  thick white keyline and a compact navy drop shadow.
- Edge anchors: overlays stay inside the 72px horizontal safe area and below the
  top-right Oristar watermark.
- Supporting detail: a green confirmation square appears only on proof beats.
- Background treatment: none. The Video 1 footage remains full-bleed and unchanged.
- Depth: a very light local scrim sits immediately behind text only; never a
  full-frame tint or grade.
- Typography: Oswald 700 for headlines, JetBrains Mono 700 for small labels.
- Motion vocabulary: `line-by-line-slide`, `kinetic-beat-slam`, and a restrained
  `success-check` pop. Entrances are fast and deterministic; held type stays still.
